**Fig. 1 | Residence time drives an accelerating conversion gain across independent batches.** **a** Batch conversion trajectories against residence time. Individual batches appear in light grey; the pooled mean ± s.d. is overlaid in blue (n = 3 batches per time point). **b** Per-hour increment for each batch, plotted at the interval midpoint. Batches are distinguished by colour, line style and marker. **c** Distribution of the per-hour increment for the 0–2 h and 3–5 h intervals (n = 6 and 9 intervals). Two-sided Welch's t-test, t = 8.41, P < 0.001. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
